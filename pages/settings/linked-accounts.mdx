# Linked Accounts

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚠️">
    Linked accounts are an experimental feature. We are working on adding
    functionality to use your Parsertime account and data in other applications.
</Callout>

Linked accounts allow you to use your Parsertime account and data in other applications.
This is an experimental feature and support is limited.

Future functionality will allow you to use your Parsertime account within a Discord bot to showcase your stats
to your friends and community. We will update this page when this feature is available.
